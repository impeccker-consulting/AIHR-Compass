Machine Learning
Patterns & Predictions
ML algorithms find hidden patterns in historical HR data to make accurate predictions — who might leave, who will perform best, or what training will work. No explicit programming needed; it learns from data.
Maturity in HRMature
Predictive Analytics — Forecasts attrition, performance dips, and hiring needs before they happen
Pattern Recognition — Detects trends across thousands of data points humans would miss
Anomaly Detection — Flags unusual pay, behaviour, or process deviations instantly
Continuous Learning — Models improve automatically as more data flows in over time

NLP
Natural Language Processing
NLP enables computers to read, understand, and interpret human language — making sense of CVs, surveys, emails, reviews, and conversations at a scale no human team could match.
Maturity in HRMature
Sentiment Analysis — Reads the emotional tone in surveys, reviews, and grievances
CV Parsing — Extracts skills, experience, and qualifications from unstructured resumes
Chatbots & Assistants — Answers employee queries in real time, 24 hours a day
Bias Detection — Identifies gendered or exclusionary language in job posts and policies

Autonomous Action

📖 AI Jargon Decoder

40+ AI terms explained in plain HR-friendly language — with analogies and real HR use cases for every term.

🔍
Machine Learning (ML)
Foundations
A way for computers to learn from data and improve over time without being explicitly programmed for every situation.
💡 Like a new manager who learns from observing patterns over months — without anyone writing a manual.
HR Application: HR use: Predicting which employees are likely to resign, ranking CVs by fit, and detecting pay equity issues.
Natural Language Processing (NLP)
Foundations
AI that enables computers to read, understand, and interpret human language — written or spoken.
💡 Like a very fast reader who can analyse 10,000 exit interview transcripts overnight and tell you the top 5 themes.
HR Application: HR use: Chatbots, CV parsing, sentiment analysis, bias detection in job descriptions.
Generative AI (GenAI)
Foundations
AI that creates new content — text, images, audio, code — based on a prompt or instruction.
💡 Like a very capable writing assistant who drafts your job descriptions, policies, and learning modules at the speed of thought.
HR Application: HR use: Writing JDs, onboarding guides, performance reviews, training content, and personalised communications.
Agentic AI
Foundations
AI that can plan and take autonomous actions across multiple steps and systems to complete a complex goal.
💡 Like an employee who is given a goal and figures out all the steps, tools, and actions needed to complete it — without being told each step.
HR Application: HR use: Running end-to-end onboarding workflows, autonomously managing recruitment pipelines, and monitoring compliance 24/7.
Computer Vision
Foundations
AI that enables computers to see, understand, and interpret images and video.
💡 Like a security guard with superhuman attention who can watch 100 camera feeds simultaneously and never blink.
HR Application: HR use: Document verification, workplace safety monitoring, diversity auditing of employer brand content.
Large Language Model (LLM)
GenAI Deep-Dive
The AI model behind tools like ChatGPT and Claude. Trained on vast amounts of text to understand and generate human language.
💡 LLM is like a supercharged autocomplete that has read virtually everything ever written — books, articles, websites, research papers.
HR Application: HR use: The engine powering most GenAI HR tools — from JD writers to policy generators to learning content creators.
Hallucination
Risks & Ethics
When an AI confidently states something that is factually wrong or completely made up.
💡 Like a brilliant but overconfident intern who fills in gaps in their knowledge with plausible-sounding guesses — without telling you they are guessing.
HR Application: HR risk: Critical — always verify AI-generated legal, policy, or factual content before distributing. GenAI can hallucinate statutory requirements or case law.
Prompt Engineering
GenAI Deep-Dive
The skill of writing clear, specific instructions to get the best output from a GenAI tool.
💡 Like knowing exactly how to brief a very capable but extremely literal colleague — the more precise you are, the better the result.
HR Application: HR use: HR teams who learn to write good prompts get dramatically better output from GenAI tools — saving time and improving quality.
Training Data
Foundations
The historical data used to teach an AI model how to make predictions or generate outputs.
💡 Like the case files and past decisions a new HR manager reads to understand how the organisation works.
HR Application: HR risk: If training data is biased (e.g. mostly male past hires), the model will perpetuate that bias. Audit training data carefully.
Algorithmic Bias
Risks & Ethics
When an AI system produces systematically unfair outcomes for certain groups — usually because it learned from biased historical data.
💡 Like a promotion committee that only promotes people who match the profile of past leaders — inadvertently excluding diverse candidates.
HR Application: HR risk: The biggest ethical risk in AI-in-HR. Most prominent in CV screening and talent scoring. Regular auditing is essential.
Predictive Analytics
ML Deep-Dive
Using historical data and statistical models to forecast what is likely to happen in the future.
💡 Like a weather forecast for your workforce — based on past patterns, you can predict storms (attrition, skill gaps) before they hit.
HR Application: HR use: Attrition prediction, workforce planning, identifying high-potential employees, and forecasting hiring needs.
Sentiment Analysis
NLP Deep-Dive
AI that reads text and determines the emotional tone — positive, negative, or neutral.
💡 Like a very empathetic colleague who can read 1,000 survey responses and tell you exactly how people are feeling — and why.
HR Application: HR use: Analysing pulse survey responses, exit interview transcripts, Glassdoor reviews, and employee feedback at scale.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
GenAI Deep-Dive
A technique that connects a GenAI model to your organisation's own documents so it gives accurate, company-specific answers.
💡 Like giving a brilliant general-knowledge assistant your company handbook — now they can answer questions specific to your organisation.
HR Application: HR use: Powers HR chatbots that give accurate policy answers rather than generic responses. Dramatically reduces hallucination risk.
Fine-Tuning
GenAI Deep-Dive
Further training a general AI model on your organisation's specific data so it performs better for your context.
💡 Like sending a generally talented new hire on a specialised course in your industry — they come back much more effective.
HR Application: HR use: Fine-tuned models for performance review writing, competency assessment, or your specific HR policies are more accurate and relevant.
Vector Database
Technical Concepts
A special type of database that stores information in a way that allows AI to find semantically similar content — not just exact keyword matches.
💡 Like a very smart filing system that understands meaning — searching for promotion finds files about advancement, career growth, and recognition even if those words are not in the search.
HR Application: HR use: Enables powerful HR knowledge bases and chatbots that find the right policy even when employees phrase questions differently.
AI Agent
Agentic AI Deep-Dive
An AI system that can perceive its environment, make decisions, use tools, and take actions autonomously to achieve a goal.
💡 Like a virtual employee who can use apps, send emails, check databases, and complete multi-step tasks — without step-by-step instructions.
HR Application: HR use: Autonomous onboarding orchestration, compliance monitoring, and end-to-end recruitment workflow management.
Human-in-the-Loop
Risks & Ethics
An AI system design where humans review, approve, or override AI decisions at key checkpoints.
💡 Like a junior analyst who does the research and draft — but a senior manager always signs off before anything goes out.
HR Application: HR best practice: Essential for high-stakes HR decisions — shortlisting, performance ratings, pay decisions, and disciplinary actions must have human review.
Explainability (XAI)
Risks & Ethics
The ability of an AI system to provide understandable reasons for the decisions or predictions it makes.
💡 Like the difference between a manager who says 'you did not get promoted — here is why with specific evidence' versus one who just says 'the algorithm decided.'
HR Application: HR requirement: Employees have a legal right in many jurisdictions to understand AI-driven decisions affecting them. Explainable AI supports fairness.
Model Drift
ML Deep-Dive
When an AI model becomes less accurate over time because the real world changes but the model was not updated.
💡 Like a market map drawn in 2019 — it was accurate then, but remote work has changed everything and the old map is misleading now.
HR Application: HR risk: Attrition models trained before COVID may be completely inaccurate now. Models must be monitored and retrained regularly.
Zero-Shot Learning
GenAI Deep-Dive
When an AI model performs well on a task it was never explicitly trained on — because it generalises from related knowledge.
💡 Like asking a brilliant generalist consultant to write a job description for a job role that did not exist when they trained — and they do a great job anyway.
HR Application: HR use: Modern LLMs can write JDs, policies, and comms for new roles without needing examples of those specific documents.
Agentic Loop
Agentic AI Deep-Dive
The cycle an AI agent runs repeatedly: perceive situation, plan action, use a tool, observe result, then repeat until goal is achieved.
💡 Like how a detective works — gather evidence, form hypothesis, investigate, find new evidence, revise hypothesis — until the case is solved.
HR Application: HR use: An onboarding agent runs this loop for each new hire — checking what is done, what is pending, taking the next action, and repeating.
Bias Audit
Risks & Ethics
A systematic review of an AI system to detect whether it produces unfair or discriminatory outcomes for specific groups.
💡 Like an external auditor reviewing your pay data for gender gaps — except here they are reviewing your AI decisions for demographic patterns.
HR Application: HR best practice: Essential for any AI used in hiring, performance, or promotion decisions. Should be done quarterly and by an independent team.
Foundation Model
GenAI Deep-Dive
A very large AI model trained on enormous amounts of data that can be adapted for many different tasks.
💡 Like a world-class generalist consultant who has studied everything — you brief them on your specific context and they immediately add value.
HR Application: HR use: GPT-4, Claude, and Gemini are foundation models. HR teams access their capabilities through tools built on top of these models.
Multimodal AI
Technical Concepts
AI that can process and generate multiple types of content — text, images, audio, and video — in the same system.
💡 Like an employee who can read a document, listen to a recording, look at a chart, and synthesise all three into a recommendation.
HR Application: HR use: Emerging — multimodal AI could analyse a video interview, the candidate CV, and psychometric test results together for a richer assessment.
Responsible AI
Risks & Ethics
A set of principles and practices to ensure AI systems are fair, transparent, safe, accountable, and respectful of human rights.
💡 Like good governance in HR — just as you have policies to ensure fair hiring, you need policies to ensure AI makes fair decisions.
HR Application: HR role: HR is uniquely positioned to lead Responsible AI adoption — because HR owns the most sensitive people decisions in the organisation.
